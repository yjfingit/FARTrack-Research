# ICASSP 2027 Submission Target

Target venue: **IEEE ICASSP 2027**.

The official ICASSP 2027 Call for Papers, accessed on 2026-08-20, specifies up
to four pages of technical content including figures and references, plus an
optional fifth page containing only references, funding acknowledgements, and a
Compliance with Ethical Standards statement. The official author-kit template
was not linked from the conference pages at that access date. The current
`spconf` files are therefore a working layout proxy, not proof of final format
compliance.

Before any submission, a human author must:

1. Download the official ICASSP 2027 author kit and replace the proxy style.
2. Re-check the page budget, anonymization, and all current submission rules.
3. Verify every citation and experimental claim against the evidence ledger.
4. Complete authorship, funding, conflict, data/code availability, and AI-use
   disclosure statements under the then-current IEEE policy.
5. Approve the final PDF. This repository contains a draft only and is not
   submission-ready.

Official sources:

- ICASSP 2027 CFP: https://2027.ieeeicassp.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/13/2019/08/ICASSP2027-CallForPapers-July1.pdf
- ICASSP 2027 editorial policies: https://2027.ieeeicassp.org/about/editorial-policies/
